About Us

ONE Water is a South African smart water technology and services company providing advanced digital, AI-enabled solutions, infrastructure services, and real-time operational performance to utilities, municipalities, and industrial clients. We help organisations reduce water losses, improve network reliability, and transform water infrastructure performance using cutting-edge digital twin ecosystems, predictive analytics, and hands-on engineering excellence.

What sets ONE Water apart is our hybrid capability — combining world-class digital platforms with physical field engineering. We don't just detect problems through satellite imagery and acoustic sensors; our specialist teams perform live pipeline repairs under pressure, with zero system shutdowns, protecting revenue and ensuring continuous service delivery.

Our mission is to enable data-driven water management, lower Non-Revenue Water (NRW), and support resilient, sustainable water services across South Africa and the broader African continent.

Vision

  • To lead the transformation of water management across Africa through intelligent digital solutions, real-time performance monitoring, and responsive field services — delivering local African expertise combined with global technology standards.

Mission

  • To deliver integrated smart water solutions that empower utilities and industrial clients with reliable infrastructure, predictive insight, and cost-effective operations — from detection through to physical repair.

Core Values

  • Innovation in water and digital technologies
  • Operational excellence and safety
  • Sustainability and resource efficiency
  • Local expertise with global standard delivery
Our African Advantage

Purpose-Built for Africa's Water Challenges

The water infrastructure challenges facing African municipalities, mines, and agricultural operators are unique. Ageing networks, high NRW rates, arid climates, and rapid urbanisation demand solutions that combine technological sophistication with deep local knowledge. ONE Water is built precisely for this.

Pan-African Reach

Operating from our Sandton headquarters, our vision and capacity extends across Sub-Saharan Africa and beyond. We are actively scaling our footprint to deliver smart water solutions continent-wide.

Local African Expertise

Our teams understand the regulatory, environmental, and infrastructural realities of operating in Africa. We adapt world-class technology to local conditions — not the other way around.

Global Technology Standards

Our Autodesk-powered digital twins, AI analytics, and satellite detection technologies meet international best practice — giving African utilities access to the same tools as their global counterparts.

Community Water Security

Water security is existential for African communities. Reducing NRW, preventing infrastructure failure, and enabling continuous supply directly improves quality of life at scale.
