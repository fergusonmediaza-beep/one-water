About Us

ONE Water is a South African smart water technology and services company providing advanced digital, AI-enabled solutions, infrastructure services, and real-time operational performance to utilities, municipalities, and industrial clients. We help organisations reduce water losses, improve network reliability, and transform water infrastructure performance using cutting-edge digital twin ecosystems, predictive analytics, and hands-on engineering excellence.

ONE Water specialises in digital transformation, operational optimisation, and advanced field services for water infrastructure. We combine world-class digital platforms with practical engineering expertise to deliver measurable performance improvements. We leverage proven global platforms adapted to local conditions.

Our mission is to enable data-driven water management, lower non-revenue water, and support resilient, sustainable water services across South Africa and beyond.

Vision

  • To lead the transformation of water management in Africa through intelligent digital solutions, real-time performance monitoring, and responsive field services.

Mission

  • To deliver integrated smart water solutions that empower utilities and industrial clients with reliable infrastructure, predictive insight, and cost-effective operations.

Core Values

  • Innovation in water and digital technologies
  • Operational excellence and safety
  • Sustainability and resource efficiency
  • Local expertise with global standard delivery
