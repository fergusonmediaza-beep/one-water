Case Studies & Proven Impact

Real-world applications demonstrating how ONE Water supports smarter, more sustainable water management across industries

Municipal

City-Wide Leak Detection & Water Loss Reduction

Aging infrastructure led to significant water loss and frequent service disruptions across a large municipal network.

Deployment of real-time pressure monitoring and AI-driven anomaly detection through the ONE Water platform enabled proactive maintenance.

Substantial reduction in non-revenue water and improved service reliability for residents.
Mining

Remote Mine Site Water Optimization

High water demand in an arid region, coupled with strict environmental compliance requirements and limited freshwater access.

Integrated water recycling controls and automated compliance reporting tailored to South African regulatory standards.

Significantly improved water reuse and consistent adherence to environmental permits.
Agriculture

Precision Irrigation for Large-Scale Farming

Inconsistent yields and inefficient water use during drought conditions on a major South African farm.

Implementation of soil moisture sensors and weather-responsive irrigation scheduling via ONE Water analytics.

Better crop performance with significantly reduced water consumption across all growing zones.
Industrial

Manufacturing Plant Water Recovery System

High operational costs from water intake and wastewater treatment at a beverage production facility.

Automated water quality tracking and process water reuse protocols integrated into daily operations.

Lower freshwater dependency, reduced treatment expenses, and stronger sustainability reporting for stakeholders.
